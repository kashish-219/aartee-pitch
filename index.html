The Guardian's five rules
  1. Pays only the verified landlord
  2. Stays under the tenant's cap
  3. Inside the payment window
  4. Not already paid this month
  5. Late fee within New York law

The agent can't go around it. Every rent wallet needs 2 of 2 signatures and has its master key disabled, and Gemini never sees a key.

0:50 to 1:25. Our core idea: the AI decides, the ledger enforces. Tenants reach their agent through iMessage with Photon, or the web app. The agents run on Vercel with Gemini and MongoDB. Every rent wallet needs two signatures: the agent signs, then our Guardian, a separate service with its own key, decodes the real transaction and checks five rules. Only then does XRPL settle it.
